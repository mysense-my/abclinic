Aging and Wrinkles at 28! – Why Everyone SHOULD Be Seeking Aesthetic Treatment as Early as Possible, According to Dr. Abby

8 December 2021webmaster

If you’re here, chances are, the small wrinkles and tiny signs of aging forming on the smile lines, neck, forehead, and eyes are bugging you. I totally get you! Who doesn’t love seeing how they look back in their early 20’s right? Youthful, radiant, glowing, and bright! Oh, the good old days!

They say sunscreen creates a difference, but what if I told you that you could still pull that off even in your 30’s and 40’s with this secret method? If you want to know my secret, scroll towards the end! BUT, if you want to learn more about wrinkles, how they form, and how to take care of wrinkles better without breaking your bank, then you might want to read through this!

I can guarantee you it’ll only take 7 minutes. Or even better, bookmark this and read it when you’re on Lunch Break or at home with a nice cup of hot tea. So, without wasting any more seconds, let’s start!

Wrinkles; who is she?

Wrinkles are skin folds formation due to repeated facial muscle contractions or muscle movement. Fine lines, however, are superficial skin indentations or crevices on the skin surface. Bet you didn’t know the difference, right? Well, both wrinkles and fine lines formation are natural skin biological processes that all living humans go through. However, some individuals may experience much severe aging process due to several aggravating factors such as environmental factors like dry weather, exposures to UV light, long direct sun exposure, hereditary process, and hormonal influenced.

Wrinkles; to treat or not?

I can say that wrinkles affect individuals differently. Depending on the person, the common yet major impact of wrinkles is loss of confidence. They often suffer from self-image issues, such as self-consciousness, embarrassment, and humiliation. But, one thing I always tell my patients; aging is a mind over matter. If you don’t mind, it doesn’t matter!

However, if you do think that treating your wrinkles can help you boost confidence and love yourself more than ever, then I support you, gorgeous! If you ask me, there are several anti-aging treatments you can ask for to combat wrinkles. The famous ones include Sublative Rejuvenation, Dermal Fillers, and Skin Booster Injections.

BUT, my personal favorite is Dermapen 4 Microneedling!

Dermapen 4 Microneedling; 6-in-1 Miracle Treatment.

The aging process can’t be stopped, but clinical skincare and aesthetic treatments are known to slow down the process of skin aging. Thus, remarkably improve the appearance of wrinkles and fine lines dramatically. And microneedling is the solution you’ve wished you’d known earlier!

Microneedling works by causing controlled trauma to the skin by making tiny micro channels onto the skin (aka poking the skin with super teeny 0 to 3 mm holes). So, the natural collagen induction process is triggered naturally by our body. At the same time, serum containing specific blends of active substances (hyaluronic acid, copper peptides, vitamin C, etc) are infused concurrently during microneedling. Meaning, the skin-loving ingredients goodies will penetrate deep into the skin much faster and better!

This allows the skin to renew faster and replace the ‘old’ skin with softer and ‘newer’ skin. The end result, in 4-6 weeks, your skin will feel like a baby’s butt; soft, supple, and healthier!

Who should get Dermapen 4 Microneedling?

Literally anyone! Especially those who suffer from:

  • Scarred skin
  • Rough texture
  • Dull skin tone
  • Sun-damaged skin
  • Large pore
  • Acne skin & acne scars
  • Pigmented skin
  • Wrinkles & fine lines
  • Aged skin

Why I LOVE Dermapen 4 Mircroneedling!

The procedure stimulates your skin to produce its own natural collagen and elastin. It makes your skin more youthful, rather than injecting an artificial substance to try to look like more youthful skin. It is a rapid treatment with minimal downtime, safe with no associated thermal tissue damage, and a much minimal risk of post-treatment pigmentary changes in darker skin.

Plus, this treatment is evidence-based. Meaning, many proven results back up this treatment!

“Microneedling or percutaneous collagen induction therapy is considered the most effective, non-invasive, inexpensive, and popular treatment modality for atrophic post-acne scars all over the world with minimal downtime, especially in patients with Fitzpatrick’s IV and V skin types.”

Dermatologic Surgery Journal October 2018;44(10):1332-1341

Simple answer, YES! Well, that is because, at my clinic, I only use the most advanced microneedling device, which is the Dermapen 4 Microneedling by Dermapen world (made in Australia). It is an advanced fractional micro-needling device that has revolutionized the treatment of certain skin disorders.

Up to 1920 micro punctures per second are delivered resulting in stimulation of new collagen and elastin production to rejuvenate the skin and correct abnormal scar collagen.
