Revolutionary Gouri Skin Booster Injection: Fountain Of Youth For 40s

11 March 2023webmaster

Can dermatologists get rid of deep wrinkles permanently?

When do wrinkles start appearing?

Believe it or not, wrinkles start appearing as early as your 20s.

During this period, you might not notice the changes, but the most common early signs of skin aging are horizontal brow wrinkles and the mid-upper forehead. Of course, at this stage, they are not serious.

But, as you reach your 30s, wrinkles tend to get deeper — most of the time caused by sun exposure and squinting. The crow’s feet are the common skin aging you can witness at this stage. Noticeable frown lines while resting often suggest the onset of other aspects of facial aging. If left unchecked, they can result in deeper frown lines and sagging skin by the 40s.

“But, can anti aging skincare effectively treat wrinkles?”

Well, unless you are using prescription-level retinoid or skincare, you are most probably not going to get noticeable results, at least not in a short period. So, what are other initiatives?

So, as KL’s top aesthetician, I — Dr Abby, will uncover one of my best anti-aging facial injectables treatments, known as Gouri.

What are Gouri’s benefits?

Gouri injectable treatment is a full-face injectable implant. I usually recommend this to male and female clients who are experiencing minor or medium levels of skin aging. This injectable treatment helps to supple, lift and enhance the appearance of their face without major deformities (swollen faces) — perfect for those who are looking for an anti-aging treatment in their 40s.

Gouri skin booster injection tackles skin aging problems, such as:

  • Facial relaxation
  • Wrinkles
  • Photoaging (skin aging caused by UV rays)
  • Creases around the nose and eyes
  • Volume loss (sagginess)

Over time, Gouri treatment benefits in:

  • Firm and tighten the facial shape
  • Enhance the face’s oval
  • Fill wrinkles (and acne scars too!)
  • Smooth skin texture
  • Regenerate the skin cells
  • Promote youthful, fresh, and timeless complexion.

But, is Gouri a filler?

Gouri is an anti-aging injection that promotes natural skin collagen renewal. It employs liquid polycaprolactone (PCL), an FDA-approved implantable biomaterial. Gouri exists in a liquid form, which means it is solubilized, biodegradable, and biocompatible.

When Gouri is injected into the skin, it will be readily and uniformly absorbed and dispersed throughout the entire face.

So, Gouri skin booster won’t stiff up your face and will produce a more “natural” result.

Is Gouri better than Profhilo?

It’s like comparing a red apple and a green one — both are healthy, delicious and nutritious, but each has its own distinctive taste.

Gouri revitalizes the skin’s collagen boost across the face using liquid PCL (polycarprolactone). When injected, it spreads organically throughout the entire face and stimulates collagen synthesis, which combats skin aging naturally.

Profhilo, on the other hand, combats dark circles and wrinkles by using hyaluronic acid to maintain moisture and promote collagen formation. It increases skin elasticity and deep hydration, as well as addresses skin laxity issues.

To find out which is best for you, come let’s have a talk and I’ll help diagnose your skin needs.

What is the best time to take a skin booster?

They say, it is never too late for a glow-up, and I stand by that!

I have clients ranging from the age of 20 – 50 who get this filler treatment for their faces. Despite skin boosters providing benefits for people of all ages, I would say the optimal time to start getting skin boosters is between your mid-twenties and forties.

Why? As previously, I have written that we begin to see a decrease in skin’s natural hyaluronic acid, collagen, and elasticity by the age of 20. Getting your skin treated early is a great way to ensure your skin is free from deep wrinkles and rough textures.

When the skin receives excellent replenishing ingredients to retain the skin’s moisture levels, you’ll be looking soft, healthy, and timeless by the age of 40!

Who doesn’t want that, right?
