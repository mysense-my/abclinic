From Saggy to Stunning: This Miraculous Treatment of Gouri Will Change Your Life!

27 July 2023webmaster

Collagen is made up 30% of our body’s protein and it’s essential for your bones, skin, hair, muscles, tendons, and ligaments. However, when we enter our mid-20s, we start losing collagen which helps to keep the skin plump and youthful. Without it, our skin would be prone to wrinkles and sagging.

While losing collagen is inevitable, there are some ways to curb those fine lines and loss of elasticity to your skin. And no, I’m not just talking about drinking water, wearing sunscreen, or leading a healthy lifestyle.

They’re great, yes. But the results aren’t as long-term. If you’re looking to give your skin a much-needed collagen boost, then I may just have the solution for you.

The Miraculous Treatment; Gouri!

NEW! From our clinic, let us introduce you to GOURI, an exciting new skin injectable that can work wonders for your skin!

When we use GOURI, it can help to stimulate collagen production deep within your skin’s dermis, giving you a beautiful and natural lift.

How Does Gouri Work?

The secret lies in its unique formulation containing a soluble version of PCL (polycaprolactone), which forms a 3D matrix when injected into the skin. This matrix activates fibroblasts, enhancing skin suppleness and delivering that much-desired face-lifting effect.

One of the most remarkable things about GOURI is that it uses PCL, a slow biodegradable biomaterial that works wonders in the human body.

By injecting it in the form of microparticles, it encourages collagen formation, making it ideal for addressing various signs of face aging such as volume loss and contour laxity. The results are not only instant but also long-lasting, giving you a beautifully natural impact that you’ll love.

Unlike traditional fillers, GOURI’s PCL-based formula can have a more widespread impact on your skin.

Dermal fillers may stimulate collagen regeneration too, but they have limitations due to their particle nature and shape. This means they’re typically used in focus areas only. On the other hand, GOURI can also be used to address specific types of acne scars, making it a versatile and effective treatment option.

If you’re looking to rejuvenate your skin, GOURI PCL Injectable could be the perfect choice for you.

Who is Gouri Suitable For?

Whether you’re in your twenties or beyond, and you want to treat skin laxity, decrease volume loss, plump laugh lines, smooth out wrinkles and lines, or lift saggy areas like jowls, GOURI can work wonders to help you achieve your goals.

At our clinic, we specialize in addressing a range of skin concerns with our treatments, including naturally lifting saggy, aging skin, improving skin elasticity for a rejuvenated facelift appearance, reducing fine lines and wrinkles, and promoting the activation of fibroblasts for the synthesis of fresh collagen. With our friendly and professional approach, we’ll help you achieve your desired results and restore your skin’s youthful radiance.

And the best part about Gouri is, it involves minimal downtime, so you can get back to enjoying life with a radiant and lifted appearance! So if you’re looking to for a collagen boost, try it out!
