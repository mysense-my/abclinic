What is Profound Matrix™ and How Does It Work?

At Dr Abby Clinic, we’re proud to offer Profound Matrix™, an advanced skin treatment that precisely addresses the diverse needs of ageing skin. Powered by Dimensional RF™ technology, Profound Matrix™ offers a multi-depth approach, treating multiple layers of the skin in one session. This makes it highly effective for individuals looking to combat a wide range of concerns such as fine lines and wrinkles, skin laxity, uneven skin texture, acne scars, and more!

Profound Matrix™ delivers radiofrequency energy through micro-needles at various depths, stimulating the body’s natural production of collagen, elastin, and hyaluronic acid. This combination results in firmer, smoother, and more radiant skin, giving you a refreshed and youthful appearance.

Experience Full Spectrum Care with Profound Matrix™ Applicators

Profound Matrix™ comes with three specialised applicators, each designed to address specific skin concerns:

Matrix Pro® Applicator

This applicator delivers short-pulse RF energy at up to three distinct depths in a single insertion. The result is the stimulation of new collagen production and a noticeable improvement in skin texture and firmness.

Sublime® Applicator

Using a combination of infrared (IR) and bipolar RF energies, the Sublime® applicator heats the skin layers, shortening aged collagen fibres to smooth wrinkles and rejuvenate the skin. This non-invasive treatment offers visible results with minimal downtime.

Sublative™ RF Applicator

Designed for skin resurfacing, the Sublative™ RF applicator delivers bipolar RF energy through an array of multi-electrode pins. It creates a fractional treatment effect that stimulates new collagen growth, resulting in smoother skin and a reduction in facial wrinkles.
