What is PRX T33 Treatment & How Can It Help You?

PRX T33 is a unique bio-revitalization treatment that combines the power of trichloroacetic acid (TCA) with hydrogen peroxide (H2o2). This innovative blend stimulates your skin’s natural regenerative processes, promoting collagen production and enhancing overall skin texture. Other than this unique blend of effectiveness, PRX T33 stands out as a favored choice for its minimally invasive nature; no needles, no pain, and no interruption in social life in the period following the treatment.

The Treatment Process

During the PRX T33 Treatment, our skilled professionals apply the solution to your skin, triggering a controlled and painless exfoliation process. This, in turn, encourages the renewal of skin cells, unveiling a fresh and rejuvenated appearance.

Benefits of PRX T33 Treatment

  • Immediate Radiance: Experience a noticeable glow and improved skin texture right after the first session.
  • Stimulated Collagen: The treatment kickstarts collagen production, reducing fine lines and promoting skin elasticity.
  • Minimised Imperfections: Say goodbye to blemishes, hyperpigmentation, and other skin irregularities.
  • No Downtime: Enjoy the benefits of a revitalized complexion without the need for extended recovery periods.
