Define & Lift with Namica Technology in Aptos Thread Lifting

At Dr Abby Clinic, we are delighted to introduce Aptos Thread Lifting with NAMICA Technology, a treatment designed to help you achieve a refreshed and youthful appearance. This approach enhances the traditional thread-lifting technique, providing a non-invasive solution to lift and tighten your skin.

NAMICA Technology in Aptos Thread Lifting enhances the effectiveness of non-invasive facial rejuvenation treatments. This approach involves a unique encapsulation method where Hyaluronic Acid (HA) molecules are coated with Polylactic Acid (PLA). This coating protects the HA during the procedure, ensuring precise delivery and controlled release to the targeted areas. By gradually releasing HA, NAMICA Technology helps maintain skin hydration and improve texture over time. The technology delivers HA in various sizes, from Nano to Micro, allowing for tailored stimulation of collagen production and overall skin firmness.

The benefits of NAMICA Technology include long-lasting results, with effects visible for up to a year, and a non-surgical solution that offers skin tightening and lifting with minimal downtime.

NAMICA Technology in Aptos Thread Lifting provides a reliable and great option for those seeking a youthful and refreshed appearance.

NAMICA Technology helps our Aptos Thread Lifting by blending Hyaluronic Acid (HA) with Polylactic Acid (PLA). This ensures that HA is released exactly where it’s needed, improving the treatment’s results.

NAMICA Technology releases hyaluronic acid (HA) gradually. This approach helps reduce scarring and promotes the growth of collagen and blood vessels over time, enhancing your skin’s texture and tone.

Determining Your Fit for Our Aptos Thread Lifting

Aptos Thread Lifting with NAMICA Technology is a versatile and effective treatment for many individuals looking to enhance their appearance. However, it’s important to determine if this procedure is right for you. Here’s a guide to help you assess your suitability:

  • Seeking Non-Invasive Rejuvenation: Aptos Thread Lifting is ideal for those who want to achieve a youthful and lifted appearance without undergoing surgery. If you prefer a non-invasive approach to facial rejuvenation, this treatment might be a good fit.
  • Addressing Mild to Moderate Skin Laxity: This procedure is particularly effective for individuals with mild to moderate sagging of the skin, especially in the face and neck areas. If you are experiencing early signs of skin laxity, such as loose skin or jowls, Aptos Thread Lifting could be beneficial.
  • Looking for Natural-Looking Results: If you desire natural-looking improvements in skin firmness and contour, Aptos Thread Lifting with NAMICA Technology offers gradual and subtle enhancements that blend seamlessly with your natural features.
  • In Good Overall Health: Candidates should be in good general health and have realistic expectations about the outcomes of the procedure. It’s important to discuss any underlying health conditions with your practitioner to ensure the treatment is appropriate for you.
  • Not Pregnant or Nursing: If you are pregnant or nursing, it is recommended to postpone cosmetic treatments until after this period. Please consult with your healthcare provider to determine the best time for your procedure.
  • Avoiding Certain Medications: If you are taking medications that could affect your skin’s healing process or increase bleeding risk, please inform your practitioner. They will guide you on any necessary adjustments or precautions.
  • Prepared for Aftercare: Successful results depend on proper aftercare. Ensure you are ready to follow the recommended post-treatment care instructions to maximise the benefits and achieve the best results.
