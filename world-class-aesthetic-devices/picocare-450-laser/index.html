What is Pico Laser & How Can It Help You?

PICOCARE is a Nd:YAG picosecond laser system used to treat pigmented lesions and multi-coloured tattoos. Its performance, speed, and ease of use make it one of the most advanced devices on the market.

Pico Laser provides better results with almost no side effects in pigmentation treatment and tattoo removal. This is because Pico Laser minimises damage to the skin by precisely targeting the spot to remove pigment. Pico Laser has a greater photomechanical effect with less thermal effect on the skin.

Skin Concerns Treated

One of the remarkable features of Pico Laser technology is its ability to provide impressive results with minimal disruption to your daily life. By delivering ultra-short pulses of energy, these lasers trigger a process known as photomechanical conversion. This process gently stimulates the skin, promoting collagen production and triggering the body’s natural healing response. The result is a rejuvenated complexion with improved texture, reduced pore size, and an overall refined appearance.

The Picocare 450 laser employs picosecond pulse durations at a wavelength of 450 nm. This specific wavelength is highly effective at targeting and breaking down pigment within the skin, such as age spots, melasma, and sunspots. Additionally, it stimulates collagen production, enhancing skin texture and reducing fine lines.

The Picocare 450 laser operates in picoseconds—trillionths of a second—allowing for extremely brief and precise bursts of light. This minimises thermal damage to surrounding tissues while effectively targeting pigment and skin imperfections. The picosecond pulses break down pigment particles efficiently, which are then naturally eliminated by the body’s lymphatic system.
