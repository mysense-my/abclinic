What is Sublime RF & How Does It Work?

Sublime RF is a non-invasive treatment that harnesses the power of technology to combat the visible signs of ageing. This treatment combines bi-polar radiofrequency (RF) energy with infrared light, offering an effective approach to skin rejuvenation and tightening, without the need for surgery or extensive downtime.

Skin Concerns Treated

Wrinkles and fine lines

Sagging or loose skin

Uneven skin tone and texture

Nasolabial Folds and Marionette Lines

Jowls and neck laxity

Dull, tired-looking skin

The Technology Behind Sublime RF

Sublime RF operates through a synergy of two technologies: bi-polar RF energy and infrared light. The bi-polar RF energy penetrates deep into the skin’s layers, creating heat in the dermis, where collagen production is most active. This heat stimulates the fibroblast cells to produce new collagen and elastin fibres, which are essential for maintaining skin’s firmness and elasticity. Over time, this increased collagen production leads to firmer, smoother skin with a noticeable reduction in fine lines and wrinkles.

The addition of infrared light further enhances the treatment’s efficacy. Infrared light works by gently heating the superficial layers of the skin, improving its overall texture and tone. This light energy helps to resurface damaged skin, reduce redness, and promote a more even complexion. Together, these technologies provide a comprehensive solution for addressing multiple signs of ageing, including sagging skin, fine lines, and uneven skin tone.

Tailored Treatment for All Skin Types

Sublime RF is designed to be safe and effective for all skin types and can be applied to various areas of the face and body, including the face, neck, chest, and abdomen. The treatment is fully customisable, allowing our LCP-certified practitioner to adjust the energy levels and treatment depth to suit your specific skin concerns and desired outcomes. This ensures you receive a personalised treatment plan that delivers optimal results.

The RF energy heats the deeper layers of the skin, stimulating collagen production and promoting a tighter, more youthful appearance. The infrared light further enhances the treatment by resurfacing damaged skin for a smoother, more even complexion.

Yes, Sublime RF is suitable for all skin types and tones. The treatment can be customised to address your specific skin concerns, whether you have dry, oily, or sensitive skin. Our expert practitioners will tailor the treatment to ensure it meets your unique needs.

Sublime RF can be used to treat various areas of the face and body, including the face, neck, chest, arms, hands, and abdomen. It is designed to target specific areas where you wish to reduce signs of ageing and improve skin quality.

The number of treatments required depends on your individual skin concerns and goals. Most patients see noticeable improvements after a series of treatments, typically spaced several weeks apart. Our practitioners will create a personalised treatment plan based on your needs and desired outcomes.

During the Sublime RF treatment, you may experience a warming sensation as the RF energy is applied. A cool gel is used to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. After the treatment, you might experience mild redness and swelling, which typically resolves within a few hours. You can resume your normal activities immediately after the session.

Sublime RF is a non-invasive procedure with minimal downtime. Common side effects include mild redness and swelling, which usually subside within a few hours. There is no need for recovery time, allowing you to return to your daily activities right away.

Results from Sublime RF can last up to twelve months, depending on your skin type and treatment plan. To maintain optimal results and continue improving skin health, we recommend follow-up treatments as part of your ongoing skincare regimen.
