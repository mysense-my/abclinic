Our Vision

At Dr Abby Clinic, we believe aesthetic medicine should be personalised, evidence-based, and focused on achieving natural, lasting results. Every treatment plan is carefully tailored to the individual—not driven by trends—and is delivered under the direct care of Dr Abrizah Ousman using clinically proven technologies and medical-grade treatments.

Beyond aesthetic medicine, Dr Abby Clinic provides a comprehensive approach to health, wellness, and healthy ageing. Our areas of expertise include:

  • Medical Aesthetic Treatments
  • Longevity & Healthy Ageing Medicine
  • Anti-Ageing & Regenerative Therapies
  • Bioidentical Hormone Replacement
  • Therapy (BHRT)
  • Female Intimate Wellness
  • Weight Management & Medical
  • Slimming Programmes
  • IV Nutritional Therapy & Wellness Optimisation

Our philosophy is simple: to help you look your best, feel your best, and age with confidence through safe, ethical, and scientifically backed medical care.

Meet TheDoctor

Dr. Abrizah Ousman

Founder & Lead Physician

Dr Abby has practised dermatology and aesthetic medicine for over two decades. She holds an MD from Universiti Sains Malaysia, a certification from the American Academy of Aesthetic Medicine, and a Diploma in Dermatology from Cardiff, UK. She is among a select group of physicians recognised by the Malaysian Ministry of Health with a Letter of Credentialing and Privilege (LCP) to practise aesthetic medicine.

Her approach favours natural, considered outcomes over dramatic change, with treatments planned with the same care whether it’s a first visit or a tenth.
