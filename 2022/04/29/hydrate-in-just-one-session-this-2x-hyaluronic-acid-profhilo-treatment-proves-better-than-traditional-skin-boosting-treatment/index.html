“Hydrate In Just ONE Session!” – This 2X Hyaluronic Acid Profhilo Treatment Proves Better Than Traditional Skin Boosting Treatment

29 April 2022webmaster

When Hyaluronic Acid is penetrated skin deep, it transforms your skin!

Known as THE MOST SOUGHT AFTER treatment since 2019 by The Times, Profhilo is the one treatment that I personally love to recommend to patients who suffer from skin elasticity and volume issues, such as wrinkles and dull-looking skin. Rather than just superficially plumping and filling the skin with injectables to lift wrinkles, Profhilo harnesses the power of Hyaluronic Acid (HA) to naturally plump, tighten, firm, and luminous the skin with no downtime. Talk about miracles!

So, Profhilo works similarly to other Hyaluronic Acid treatments and skincare products, right? Well, no! This rehydrating and re-texturing skin remodeling treatment contains one of the highest concentrations of HA on the market. Instead of ‘applying’ HA, this treatment ‘drenches’ your skin with HA and lets it soak it up from deep epidermal layers!

But, how can you get this treatment? Well, there are certain things you need to know.

Is HA natural or superficial?

Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a substance that naturally occurs in our body, mostly in the skin, eyes, and joints. About half of the HA within our body can be found in the skin. It binds itself to water molecules, keeping the skin hydrated and fresh-looking, which implies its main function in the skin is hydration. Therefore, dermatologists and aestheticians use HA to promote skin health and anti-aging. Generally, typical applications of HA can moisturize dry flaking dehydrated skin.

How effective is HA in combating wrinkles?

One of the main benefits of HA for the skin is the ability to rejuvenate the dermis layer of the skin. It has the ability and the potential to fill up fine lines, providing an even and smoother skin texture, whilst ultimately combating premature aging when practiced regularly.

Another key component of HA is its ability to hydrate & lock moistures deep within the skin. With a well-hydrated canvas, your complexion will appear much healthier, brighter, velvety, and florid. Such a good thing for those who wear makeup. Moreover, HA stimulates skin elasticity, allowing flaccidity and a firm complexion which, in a long run, can reduce fine lines over the face.

How does Profhilo treatment harness the power of 2X Hyaluronic Acid?

Most days, I refer to Profhilo as an injectable moisturizer to my clients. It contains a very high concentration of HA (32 milligrams) that is designed to specifically target elasticity and volume issues, such as wrinkle, fine lines, crow’s feet, smiles lines, and facial lines. In 3-5 days of treatment, Profhilo mimics the skin’s natural volume and smooths out skin texture to its natural form.

Over the next few weeks, Profhilo stimulates collagen and improves the production of your natural HA over time, producing a secondary effect of skin tightening. This impressively improves dull skin tone, increases hydration for dry skin, and reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles better than cosmetic anti-aging cream.

In conclusion, the quality of the skin improves significantly from day 3 until the next 6 months after treatment.

Why is Profhilo different from traditional skin boosters?

Profhilo improves skin quality naturally rather than superficially adding volume. The treatment process, effects, and technology are very different from traditional skin booster treatments. The main difference is due to a unique HA molecule called the Hybrid Cooperative Complex (HCC), which has a combination of both low and high molecular weight HA.

Who should get Profhilo treatment?

Generally, I would suggest Profhilo to clients who are blossoming into their 30s who do not feel comfortable with the laxity of their skin. If you are concerned about premature aging in places like the forehead, neck, or under the eyes, you are definitely a good candidate for this HA boost treatment.
