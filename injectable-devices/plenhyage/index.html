What is Plenhyage & How Does It Work?

Plenhyage is an innovative skin booster designed to do more than simply hydrate. It is made with polynucleotides (PN), purified DNA fragments that have powerful regenerative properties. These molecules work deep within the skin to repair, restore, and rejuvenate, making it especially effective for concerns such as fine lines, acne scars, pigmentation, and loss of elasticity.

Healing the Skin in Cellular Level

Plenhyage focuses on healing and regeneration at the cellular level. Once injected, it stimulates fibroblasts (the cells responsible for collagen and elastin production), increases natural hydration, and encourages tissue repair. This results in smoother, firmer skin with improved texture, reduction in wrinkles, scars, and pigmentation, and healthier, more youthful-looking skin from within.

Because Plenhyage improves the quality of your skin over time, the results are natural, subtle, and long-lasting, giving you not just healthier skin but also renewed confidence in your appearance.

Plenhyage is a skin booster made from polynucleotides (PN) — purified DNA fragments that help repair and regenerate skin. Unlike fillers, it doesn’t add volume but works at a cellular level to improve skin quality, texture, and overall health.

While fillers restore volume and Profhilo mainly hydrates, Plenhyage goes deeper. It encourages your skin to heal, repair, and rebuild itself, making it especially effective for concerns like scars, pigmentation, and sun damage.

Plenhyage is suitable for fine lines, wrinkles, acne scars, pigmentation, dullness, loss of elasticity, and even tired-looking under-eyes. It’s designed to treat the skin itself, not just the surface.

Yes. Plenhyage is clinically proven and performed only by our LCP-certified doctors. The polynucleotides are purified to the highest standard, ensuring excellent safety and tolerance.

You may feel a slight pinch during the injections, but a numbing cream is applied beforehand to keep you comfortable. Most patients describe it as very tolerable.

For best results, we usually recommend a course of 2–4 sessions, spaced a few weeks apart. Your doctor will create a plan tailored to your skin’s needs.

Many patients notice improvements in hydration and glow within weeks. The regenerative effects — like smoother texture and reduced pigmentation — continue to build over the following months.

Results typically last 6–12 months, depending on your skin and lifestyle. Maintenance treatments can help prolong the benefits.

Downtime is minimal. You may have mild redness or tiny bumps at the injection sites, but these usually settle within 24–48 hours. Most patients return to normal activities the next day.
