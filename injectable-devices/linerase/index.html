What is Linerase & How Does it Work?

Linerase is designed to rejuvenate and restore your skin’s vitality. This injectable therapy utilises a unique form of collagen known as Heterologous Type I Collagen (HTIC). Rest assured, it is completely sterile and safe for use. Linerase is particularly suitable for adults experiencing skin thinning, sagging, or reduced elasticity due to factors such as injuries or congenital conditions.

By enhancing your skin’s natural rejuvenation process, Linerase works to regenerate and repair the connective tissue beneath the surface. This creates an ideal environment for new collagen formation, leading to improved skin structure. The outcome is firmer, more elastic skin with a healthier, more radiant appearance.

Healthier Scalp and Stronger Hair with Linerase

Linerase Collagen (HTIC) offers a revitalising approach to hair and scalp health by stimulating collagen production in the deeper layers of the scalp. This treatment provides essential structural support to hair follicles, creating a robust foundation for healthy hair growth.

With gentle, targeted injections of Heterologous Type I Collagen, Linerase helps restore strength and elasticity to the scalp’s connective tissue. This process nourishes and fortifies weakened follicles, improving the quality of each hair strand and encouraging greater density over time. By increasing collagen levels in the scalp, Linerase also hydrates and revitalises the scalp environment, reducing dryness and creating an optimal setting for hair resilience.

Linerase is ideal for individuals experiencing hair thinning, weak follicles, or mild hair loss, offering a natural solution to support healthier, stronger hair from the root upwards.
