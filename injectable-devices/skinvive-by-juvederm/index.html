What is SKINVIVE and How Does It Work?

SKINVIVE by JUVÉDERM® is an injectable treatment specifically designed to improve the quality of your skin. Unlike traditional fillers that add volume, SKINVIVE enhances skin smoothness, hydration, and radiance, focusing on the cheeks. As the first FDA-approved hyaluronic acid microdroplet injectable, it provides a unique solution for achieving naturally glowing, refreshed skin.

The First and Only Hyaluronic Acid Microdroplet Injectable

SKINVIVE uses a proprietary hyaluronic acid gel, delivered through microdroplet injections into the skin’s surface. The hyaluronic acid in SKINVIVE attracts and retains moisture, providing long-lasting hydration from within. This boost in hydration smooths out fine lines and improves overall skin texture, leaving your cheeks looking plump, fresh, and revitalised.

This treatment is suitable for adults aged 21 and older, delivering subtle yet impactful results that last up to six months. With SKINVIVE, you can achieve a healthier, more luminous complexion without changing your facial contours.

SKINVIVE by JUVÉDERM® is a unique injectable treatment that hydrates and smooths the skin, enhancing its radiance. It is the first and only hyaluronic acid microdroplet injectable that improves skin texture and hydration in the cheek area.

SKINVIVE is suitable for adults aged 21 and above who are looking to improve skin hydration and smoothness. It is best suited for individuals with healthy skin, though a consultation with Dr Abby is necessary to determine your eligibility.

Many patients notice visible improvements in their skin’s hydration and smoothness immediately after treatment. Full results typically become visible within a few days as the product settles into the skin.

SKINVIVE is generally well-tolerated. Dr Abby will use a fine needle to inject the hyaluronic acid, and the treatment area may be numbed beforehand to ensure comfort. Some patients experience mild discomfort or a slight pinching sensation.

Side effects are typically mild and short-lived, such as redness, swelling, or bruising at the injection site. These effects usually subside within a few days. There is no downtime required, so you can resume your regular activities right after the treatment.

Most patients see great results after just one treatment. However, depending on your individual skin needs and goals, Dr Abby may recommend follow-up treatments to maintain optimal results.
