What is Exosome Cellresome & How Does it Work?

At Dr Abby Clinic, we pride ourselves on offering treatments that deliver remarkable skin rejuvenation. One of our latest offerings is the Exosome Cellresome, designed to revitalise and restore your skin from within.

Exosome Cellresome utilises tiny extracellular vesicles that contributes in skin health. These naturally occurring particles are derived from sources such as salmon testis and milk exosomes, and they are rich in proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids, which are essential for skin repair.

When applied to the skin, Exosome Cellresome enhances the delivery of vital nutrients and growth factors, promoting skin healing and supporting cell turnover. This treatment improves skin elasticity, texture, and hydration, effectively addressing common concerns such as fine lines, wrinkles, uneven skin tone, and hyperpigmentation.

Exosome Cellresome works in harmony with your skin’s natural processes to stimulate collagen production, reduce inflammation, and protect against environmental stressors. Whether you seek a brighter, smoother complexion or wish to maintain youthful-looking skin, Exosome Cellresome offers a safe and effective solution for long-lasting results.

Exosome Cellresome for Hair

Exosome Cellresome offers numerous benefits for hair restoration and rejuvenation. This treatment addresses hair loss, thinning, and scalp health by targeting the root causes of hair follicle damage.

With its blend of active molecules, including Salmon Testis Extract and Milk Extract, Exosome Cellresome encourages the recovery of damaged hair follicles and stimulates new hair growth. Infused with natural ingredients like Centella Asiatica, Houttuynia Cordata, and Soybean Seed Extract, this treatment nourishes the scalp, strengthens the hair shaft, and enhances the overall hair growth cycle.

By delivering essential nutrients directly to the scalp, Exosome Cellresome helps increase hair density, reduce hair loss symptoms, and support the growth of thicker, healthier hair. Its unique formulation also works to restore the scalp’s natural balance, reducing inflammation and creating a healthy environment for sustained hair growth.
