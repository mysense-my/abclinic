What is Novuma & How Does It Work?

Novuma is an advanced, collagen-stimulating injectable designed to naturally rejuvenate your skin. Unlike traditional fillers that only add volume, Novuma goes a step further by encouraging your skin to renew itself from within.

Its unique formulation combines Calcium Hydroxyapatite (CaHA) microspheres with a Carboxymethyl Cellulose (CMC) gel. The CMC gel provides an immediate lifting and smoothing effect, while the CaHA microspheres act as a scaffold, stimulating your skin to produce fresh collagen and elastin over time.

The result? Skin that looks firmer, smoother, and more radiant, not just for today, but for months to come. With results that can last up to 12–24 months, Novuma offers a subtle, natural enhancement that supports your confidence and restores your youthful glow.

Novuma is a collagen-stimulating injectable treatment made with Calcium Hydroxyapatite (CaHA) and Carboxymethyl Cellulose (CMC). It restores volume instantly while encouraging your skin to naturally produce new collagen for longer-lasting rejuvenation.

Unlike fillers that only replace lost volume, Novuma works deeper by stimulating your skin to create fresh collagen. This means results are more natural, longer-lasting, and improve over time.

Novuma is effective for fine lines, wrinkles, sagging skin, loss of firmness, deep folds, volume loss, and even early ageing in areas such as the jawline, neck, décolletage, and hands.

You’ll notice an immediate lifting and smoothing effect after treatment, with further improvements appearing gradually over the following weeks as your skin produces new collagen.

Results typically last between 12 to 24 months, depending on your skin, lifestyle, and treatment area. Many patients love that the results are both subtle and long-lasting.
