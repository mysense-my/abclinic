Understanding the Men Wellness Program

The AB Signature Men Wellness Program is a hormone optimization program designed to help men address the effects of declining testosterone — low energy, reduced muscle mass, mood changes, and reduced libido. Delivered on a half-yearly basis with blood test assessments and consultations built in, it’s structured for consistent, monitored results rather than a one-time fix. For men looking to address specific concerns further, add-on programs such as BTL Vanquish Me (belly fat reduction), Mounjaro slimming injections, and Exosome ASCE hair loss treatment can be paired with the core program.
