What is Body Contouring & Cellulite Treatment & How Does it Work?

Body contouring and cellulite treatments are designed to improve the shape and appearance of the body by reducing localised fat deposits and addressing cellulite. By addressing excess fat, loose skin, and cellulite, the treatment helps you to achieve a sculpted and toned figure.

Here at Dr. Abby Clinic, we employ a non-surgical and minimally invasive procedure to help remove excess body fat and cellulite. Our doctor will assess each patient’s condition, employing a treatment that is personalised according to their skin type, concerns, and overall health.
