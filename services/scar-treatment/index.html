What is Scar Treatment & How Does It Work?

Scar treatment refers to various medical and cosmetic procedures aimed at reducing the visibility of scars and stretch marks on the skin. Scars are common skin concerns that can result from different causes, such as injury, surgery, rapid growth, or hormonal changes.

At Dr Abby Clinic, we believe that not all scar treatments are the same. Hence, our doctor will develop the best plan to suit each individual’s skin type and the nature of the scars or stretch marks.
