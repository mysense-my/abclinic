Achieve Youthful Skin in a Single Session: The Wonders of Profound Matrix™

18 September 20248wiser

Maintaining our youth takes time, effort, and definitely money. With the right lifestyle and skincare routine, such a thing is not impossible but one way or another, ageing will catch up sooner than later. When that happens, should we turn to going under the knife to lift and tighten the skin?

The good news is, that there are ways to achieve radiant, youthful skin without the need for invasive surgery! With Profound Matrix™, it is possible. This advanced treatment offers a non-surgical solution that delivers impressive results in just one session. Combining advanced technology with expert precision, Profound Matrix™ provides a safe and effective way to rejuvenate your skin.

If you’re curious to know how this device can firm and rejuvenate your skin, then continue reading! We’ll share all you need to know about Profound Matrix™.

What is Profound Matrix™?

Profound Matrix™ is an RF microneedling device, expertly engineered to address the diverse needs of skin throughout the ageing journey. This device uses Dimensional RF™ technology to offer a versatile, multi-depth treatment in a single insertion, making it an effective solution for a broad range of skin concerns.

Experience Full Spectrum Care with Profound Matrix™ Applicators

Profound Matrix™ features three specialised applicators, each tailored to target specific skin issues:

Matrix Pro® Applicator: This applicator delivers short-pulse RF energy at up to three distinct depths within the skin. By stimulating new collagen production, it enhances skin texture and firmness, resulting in a more youthful and refined appearance.

Sublime® Applicator: Combining infrared (IR) and bipolar RF energies, the Sublime® applicator heats the skin layers to shorten aged collagen fibres. This process smooths wrinkles and rejuvenates the skin, providing visible results with minimal downtime.

Sublative™ RF Applicator: Designed for advanced skin resurfacing, the Sublative™ RF applicator uses bipolar RF energy through multi-electrode pins to create a fractional treatment effect. This stimulates new collagen growth, leading to smoother skin and a noticeable reduction in facial wrinkles.

Developed by Candela Medical, Profound Matrix™ adheres to the highest standards of safety and efficacy. Its innovative technology and specialised applicators ensure reliable and effective results, making it a trusted choice for non-surgical skin improvement.

How Profound Matrix™ Works

Microneedles and RF Energy

Profound Matrix™ utilises a sophisticated combination of microneedling and radiofrequency (RF) energy to rejuvenate the skin. During the procedure, microneedles create precise micro-injuries in the skin. These micro-injuries trigger the skin’s natural healing process. Simultaneously, RF energy is delivered through the microneedles, penetrating deeper layers of the skin. This dual-action stimulates the production of collagen, elastin, and hyaluronic acid, leading to tighter, smoother, and more youthful skin.

Precision and Control

Profound Matrix™ is engineered with high precision and control to deliver RF energy accurately and efficiently. The device’s advanced features enable precise energy delivery to targeted skin layers, optimising treatment outcomes while minimising discomfort. This meticulous control ensures that the skin receives the exact amount of energy needed to stimulate natural rejuvenation processes, resulting in effective and comfortable treatments.

Key Benefits of Profound Matrix™ for Youthful Skin!

Youthful Skin in One Session

Profound Matrix™ offers remarkable skin rejuvenation with just a single treatment session. The combination of microneedling and radiofrequency energy delivers visible improvements in skin texture and firmness, making it an efficient choice for those seeking quick and effective results.

Non-Surgical and Minimally Invasive

Enjoy the benefits of a rejuvenated appearance without the need for surgery. Profound Matrix™ is a minimally invasive procedure that provides skin tightening and smoothing with minimal downtime. This makes it an ideal option for individuals looking to enhance their skin without the need for extensive recovery.

Addresses Multiple Concerns

This versatile treatment effectively targets a range of skin concerns, including wrinkles, sagging, loss of elasticity, and uneven skin tone. Whether you’re dealing with fine lines or more pronounced signs of ageing, Profound Matrix™ can address these issues comprehensively, offering a holistic approach to skin rejuvenation.

Suitable for Various Skin Types and Ages

Profound Matrix™ is designed to be adaptable to different skin types and ages. Its flexible treatment options can be customised to meet individual needs, ensuring that each patient receives a tailored approach for optimal results.

Long-Lasting Results
The stimulation of collagen and elastin through Profound Matrix™ leads to enduring improvements in skin quality. The results of the treatment can last for an extended period, thanks to the ongoing benefits of increased collagen and elastin production, which helps maintain a youthful and vibrant appearance over time.
